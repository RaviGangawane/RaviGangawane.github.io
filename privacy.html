Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited analytics information this portfolio may collect and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Information collected

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to collect usage information such as pages visited, approximate visit duration, interactions, referral source and general device or browser information. Custom events may record actions such as project views, resume downloads and contact-link clicks. Form contents are not sent to Google Analytics.

Cookies and devices

Google Analytics may place or read analytics cookies after you select Accept. These help distinguish visits and understand site usage. Device category, operating system, browser type and screen characteristics may also be processed.

Approximate location and traffic source

Analytics may derive an approximate country, region or city from network information and report whether a visit came from a search engine, social profile, referring website or direct navigation. It does not provide an exact home address.

Purpose

Analytics is used to understand which pages are useful, measure portfolio performance, improve navigation and content, and evaluate general traffic sources. It is not used to identify visitors personally or sell personal information.

Your choices

Analytics does not load unless you accept it. Select Reject to use the portfolio without analytics. You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings or use a browser privacy feature. Changing this site’s preference stops future analytics loading on your next page view.

Google Analytics

Google processes analytics information under its own terms and privacy practices. Browser extensions, including Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on where supported, can provide an additional opt-out mechanism.
