AI product case study

AI-Enabled Angular Frontend for Document Discovery

A retrieval-augmented interface concept that helps users find relevant information across document-heavy workflows and verify answers through clear citations.

AngularOpenAI APINode.jsFastAPISemantic Search

Business problem

Users needed a clearer way to discover relevant information across document-heavy workflows without manually opening and comparing many sources.

My contribution

Owned frontend UX exploration and AI integration design, including response states, source presentation, error recovery and secure service boundaries.

Architecture decisions

  • Retrieval before generation to ground responses in source content.
  • Citations displayed with answers to support verification.
  • Server-side AI credentials and controlled API boundaries.
  • Explicit loading, empty, partial and failure states.

Technology

Angular, TypeScript, OpenAI API, Node.js, FastAPI, semantic search and a vector-store-compatible retrieval layer.

Result

The exploration validated a focused interaction model for context-aware document discovery and workflow assistance. Exact production metrics are not published because the work is presented as a product exploration.
